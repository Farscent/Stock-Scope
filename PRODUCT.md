# StockScope

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Python, Streamlit, Plotly, pandas, yfinance, Jupyter and pytest, as specified in the project brief.

## Users
Recruiters and analysts reviewing a reproducible financial data analytics portfolio.

## Product Purpose
Explore stock prices and quarterly company revenue, preserving Tesla and GameStop as the primary research examples.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Price selection, date filtering, price and volume charts, quarterly revenue, comparison, CSV export and methodology. Custom tickers support prices only. Financial availability must be explicit; no fabricated data, predictions or trading advice.

## Evidence on Hand
The original 67-cell IBM-inspired notebook is available locally. No deployment or verified dashboard screenshot exists at project start.

## Product Principles
Preserve the revenue research question. Keep daily and quarterly frequencies separate. Compute findings from observations. Report actual dates and failures.
